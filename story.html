OUR STORY

A reading salon, with room to grow.

The story of building Hotwind is also a story of listening, testing ideas and learning to change direction.

✳

WHY I STARTED

A more open place to connect.

I started Hotwind for university students who wanted a more open, sincere place to read, exchange ideas and connect beyond formal coursework.

Over time, reading became one entry point to a wider programme: public dialogue, peer learning, creative health communication and informal connection.

HOW IT EVOLVED

An idea can change as you learn.

2024.01

Start with a reading salon

I started Hotwind for university students who wanted a more open, sincere place to read, exchange ideas and connect beyond formal coursework.

↗Begin with a need you can see.
2024.04

Test a bigger idea

An innovation project became a way to explore the relationship between reading, peer support and youth development.

↗An early idea is something to test.
2024.07

Reflect, then rebuild

Recognising the limits of student-led psychological support, we repositioned the community around dialogue, learning and public engagement, and rebuilt the team around this direction.

↗Listening also means changing direction.
2025

Become a connector

The community connected students with academic, cultural, public-interest and life-science communities across Chongqing and Wuhan.

↗Build connections around shared questions.

A TURNING POINT

Learning what we could responsibly offer.

The early innovation-project stage explored reading and peer support. Recognising the professional limits of student-led psychological support led us to focus on learning, dialogue and public engagement.
