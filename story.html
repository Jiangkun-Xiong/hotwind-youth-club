OUR STORY

A reading salon, with room to grow.

The story of building Hotwind is also a story of listening, testing ideas and learning to change direction.

✳

WHY I STARTED

A more open place to connect.

I started Hotwind to offer university students a more open space to read, exchange ideas and meet beyond formal coursework.

Over time, reading became one entry point to a wider programme: public dialogue, peer learning, creative health communication and informal connection.

HOW IT EVOLVED

An idea can change as you learn.

2024.01

Start with a reading salon

I started Hotwind to offer university students a more open space to read, exchange ideas and meet beyond formal coursework.

↗Begin with a need you can see.
2024.04

Test a bigger idea

An innovation project became a way to explore the relationship between reading, peer support and youth development.

↗An early idea is something to test.
2024.07

Reflect, then rebuild

After reviewing early ideas about peer support, we refocused the community on reading, discussion and public engagement. Peer exchange remained distinct from professional psychological services.

↗Listening also means changing direction.
2025

Explore wider connections

We explored contacts with academic, cultural, public-interest and life-science groups in Chongqing and Wuhan.

↗Build connections around shared questions.

A TURNING POINT

Learning what we could responsibly offer.

The early innovation-project stage explored ideas about reading and peer support. The revised scope focused on learning, dialogue and public engagement, with a clear boundary from professional psychological services.

How we explored students’ needs↗

One lesson from building the community was to test ideas, listen to feedback and adapt when a plan needed to change.
