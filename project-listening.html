What would students want a community to help them explore?

The planning brief asked whether campus activities supported meaningful connection and different kinds of personal growth. The aim was to let students’ needs inform future activities and the organisation’s structure.

How the idea took shape

01

Define the questions

Identify two themes: meaningful social connection and multidimensional growth.

02

Explore ways to listen

Prepare questionnaire ideas and plans for conversations with students from different disciplines and years.

03

Inform future design

Use the questions to reconsider activities, participation and the community’s operating model.

My contribution

I helped frame the questions and connect the planning work to Hotwind’s development. The material is presented as exploratory planning rather than a completed research study.

WHAT I TOOK FROM IT

A useful question can change a programme. These materials do not establish an achieved sample size, validated scale or measured impact; draft literature-summary numbers are not reported as Hotwind’s findings.
