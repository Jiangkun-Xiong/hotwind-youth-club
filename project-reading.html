READING & CONNECTION

Reading, shared discussion and collective writing

Reading salons and the Changliu programme combined individual reading, online check-ins and group discussion.

Photographs document selected sessions. Programme posters describe the intended format; the founder reports around 20 weeks of shared reading overall.

What if reading were a starting point for connection?

I wanted a place where students could exchange ideas beyond coursework and have conversations with enough time and trust to go deeper.

Formats recorded in the materials

01

Read and keep a record

Programme posters invited participants to choose a book, record their progress and share short reading reflections.

02

Exchange ideas

The 2024 guide proposed online voice conversations and an in-person discussion at the end of the reading cycle.

03

Write and share together

The May 2025 materials describe combining participants’ writing into a shared work. Photographs record the closing sharing session.

My contribution

I founded the community and coordinated its development with the core team, helping turn informal interest into a recurring programme.

WHAT I TOOK FROM IT

Repeated opportunities to read and discuss were central to the programme’s design. Its duration is not evidence of participant retention or measured learning gains.
