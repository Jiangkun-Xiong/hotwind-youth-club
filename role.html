MY ROLE

I started it. We built it.

My role was to help turn a small idea into a continuing community, while learning when to lead, listen and adapt.

✳

JIANGKUN XIONG · FOUNDER & TEAM LEAD

Building something that people could take part in.

I founded Hotwind in January 2024 and led a nine-member core team. Together we organised more than 30 activities and sustained an online reading programme for around 20 weeks.

The programme, photographs and activities reflect the work of many contributors. The responsibilities below describe my contribution within that collective effort.

01 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Set the direction

Founded Hotwind and helped its purpose evolve from a reading salon into a wider community for dialogue, learning and public engagement.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Listen and adapt

Reflected on the limits of student-led psychological support and helped reposition the project around a more appropriate scope.

03 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Bring the team together

Led a nine-member core team, sharing responsibilities and coordinating activities and collaborators over time.

04 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Turn ideas into experiences

Helped develop reading programmes, interactive health communication and open discussions, including the three featured cases.

WHAT CHANGED IN ME

A better listener. A more reflective organiser.

Building Hotwind taught me that meaningful engagement often starts small: a space where people feel comfortable enough to speak, question and contribute.

See the work and its limits↗
Listening, testing ideas and adapting when something does not work.
A lesson I carry into research and collaboration.

A little curiosity. A lot of possibility.

Reading. Dialogue. Possibility. A community built by young people, together.
