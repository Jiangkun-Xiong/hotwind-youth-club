## Specific corrections

- Replace the later presentation label “Medical Mystery Night” with a descriptive label. The event text schedules an afternoon session. The booklet cover uses “逃之药天”, while the promotional text uses “逃之药夭”; the website does not silently choose a definitive title or invent an official English title.
- Translate “不用排名靠前” as “You don’t have to be a top-ranked student to be heard”, not “rank first”.
- Match English “helped plan … with the team” to Chinese “共同参与策划”, avoiding the stronger “I designed” when the intended contribution is collaborative.
- Describe “医学狼人杀” as a medical-themed social deduction game, not a diagnostic game implying clinical skills training.
- Use pregnancy-related wording where the source discusses pregnancy, rather than expanding every prompt to reproductive health generally.
- Translate “打压式教育” in context as discouraging or belittling messages in family upbringing, not the unnatural phrase “discouraging education” or an unassessed clinical diagnosis.
- Show around 20 weeks as a founder-reported cumulative estimate across reading cycles. The 2024 guide describes one four-week cycle; the May poster describes one three-week cycle.
- Present personal reflections as reflections, not fabricated direct quotations.
- Separate primary event materials from the founder’s later retrospective presentation artwork.
- Keep proposed collaborations and research planning distinct from completed partnerships and study findings.

## Content and privacy boundaries

The public site does not publish the source ZIP, literature-search PDFs, draft questionnaire scores, unverified sample statistics or private chat records. Old notices remain historical records, and their dates, QR codes, venue details and fees are not presented as current registration or payment offers. The website does not verify old QR destinations.

Photographs and posters were supplied for the project. Files alone cannot establish every photographer’s licence, participant’s portrait permission or third-party artwork permission. The publisher remains responsible for confirming the relevant permissions and handling rights-holder requests. Displaying institutional names or logos does not establish endorsement of this website.

## Basic technical checks

- No registration, survey, payment or contact form; no analytics script, advertising embed or external image URL in the reviewed site code.
- Local browser storage is used for the English/Chinese preference; the hosting provider’s own access records are outside that code review.
- Media paths are restricted to local image files; invalid paths, remote thumbnails and unsafe CSS-position strings are rejected or normalised.
- HTML text and captions are escaped; the published pages use a self-only script policy and prohibit script-driven network connections, object embeds and form submission.
- Local page/asset links, bilingual source labels, the corrected wording and media-path handling are checked by the repository scripts.

Run `node scripts/check.mjs` and `node scripts/check-content.mjs` after rebuilding. A passing check does not itself establish copyright, portrait permission, legal compliance or truth of an unsupported claim.
