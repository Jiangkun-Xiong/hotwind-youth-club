You don’t have to be a top-ranked student to be heard.

Peer Voices invited students to share learning approaches, everyday experiences and reflections without requiring a high academic ranking.

The archived posters and notes describe the invitation to share and proposed topics. They do not provide a participant count or an assessment of personal growth.

Whose experience gets to count as something worth sharing?

The programme notes emphasise that students may have useful experiences to share beyond conventional measures of academic achievement.

The invitation in the programme notes

01

Share ways of learning

The invitation welcomed personal learning approaches and practical habits.

02

Talk about experience

Everyday experiences, interests and reflections on university life were among the suggested topics.

03

Broaden the invitation

The notes explicitly welcomed contributions without making academic rank a condition for speaking.

My contribution

I created the platform with the community, making room for students to share what they had learned and how they were growing.

WHAT I TOOK FROM IT

Meaningful engagement can start with a simple invitation: your experience is worth listening to.
