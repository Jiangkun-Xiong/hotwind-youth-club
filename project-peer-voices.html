Whose experience gets to count as something worth sharing?

A community can hear from a wider range of people when conventional measures of achievement are not the only invitation to speak.

How the idea took shape

01

Share a learning strategy

Students had space to explain ways of learning through their own experience.

02

Talk about real experience

Social experiences and personal reflections could become material for peer learning.

03

Broaden the invitation

The platform welcomed perspectives beyond conventional measures of academic achievement.

My contribution

I created the platform with the community, making room for students to share what they had learned and how they were growing.

WHAT I TOOK FROM IT

Meaningful engagement can start with a simple invitation: your experience is worth listening to.
