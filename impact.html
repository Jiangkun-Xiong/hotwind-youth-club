IMPACT & EVIDENCE

What we made possible, together.

A record of activities, sustained participation and lessons from building a student-led community.

↗
30+activities organisedAcross reading, dialogue and health communication
20weeks of shared readingA sustained space for learning together
9people in the core teamA small team with shared responsibilities

Programme totals are reported by the founder. The archive documents selected activities rather than every session.

01

More ways to take part

Reading, story-making, a medical mystery, student debate and informal gatherings offered different entry points to community life.

02

A continuing invitation

The shared-reading programme created a recurring opportunity to learn and exchange ideas over around 20 weeks.

03

A wider range of voices

Peer Voices made room for students to share strategies and experiences beyond conventional measures of academic success.

04

Connections across interests

The community explored connections with academic, cultural, public-interest and life-science communities in Chongqing and Wuhan.

WHAT THESE MATERIALS SHOW

A community in practice, with room to learn.

Posters, photographs and activity plans show what we organised and how we approached it. They do not by themselves demonstrate a measured improvement in health, wellbeing or long-term social outcomes.

Exploratory research documents are shown as planning work. Proposed collaborations and draft literature summaries are not presented as completed partnerships or original study results.

A little curiosity. A lot of possibility.

Reading. Dialogue. Possibility. A community built by young people, together.
