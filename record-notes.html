ABOUT THIS ARCHIVE

About these records

This is an archive of a student-led project and selected materials from 2024–2025.

↗

What each record supports

Activity photographs document selected moments. Posters, notices and plans describe topics and intended formats; they do not by themselves establish that every proposed segment took place. Exploratory research materials are labelled as planning.

The totals of 30+ activities, around 20 weeks of shared reading and nine core team members come from the founder’s account. This site contains selected examples, not a complete activity or attendance register. The reading total is not a claim of 20 uninterrupted weeks or a measure of participant retention.

Personal contributions are described separately from team work. The archive does not claim measured improvements in knowledge, behaviour, health or wellbeing.

Language and activity names

English descriptions summarise the Chinese source materials. Descriptive English labels help readers navigate the site; they are not presented as original English event titles. Original wording remains visible in the archived posters.

The retrospective project overview includes later English presentation artwork alongside event photographs. The case pages use the original posters and plans for their descriptions.

Historical notices

Dates, venue details, prices and QR codes in old notices belong to those historical materials. This website does not accept registrations, deposits or payments and does not verify the current destinations of old QR codes.

Health and discussion content

The tuberculosis mystery uses a fictional story, including a fictional missing medicine. Its game format is not clinical diagnostic training. Discussion prompts about pregnancy and family upbringing are presented as topics, not medical conclusions or consensus statements.

This archive does not offer individual medical advice, diagnosis, counselling or psychological treatment.

Images, names and attribution

Photographs and posters are displayed as selected project materials. Their inclusion does not grant visitors permission to reuse them. Third-party marks and institutional names remain associated with their respective owners; their appearance records the source material and does not imply endorsement of this website.

Corrections or rights-related concerns can be raised with the site publisher through the linked GitHub profile. Please do not post private personal information in public repository discussions.

What the site code stores

The site has no registration, survey, payment or contact form. Its code stores the English/Chinese language choice locally in your browser so the preference can be shared with the portfolio site. It includes no analytics script or advertising embed.

GitHub hosts the site and handles requests to its infrastructure separately. This description concerns the site code and is not a statement that the hosting provider keeps no access records.
