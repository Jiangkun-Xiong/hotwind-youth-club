How could health education invite people to participate?

Tuberculosis is a medical topic, but the activity needed an accessible starting point for participants without specialist knowledge. A mystery provided a shared question to explore.

How the idea took shape

01

Enter the story

An original medical mystery introduced a shared problem through a fictional narrative.

02

Think together

A diagnostic-themed social game brought discussion and interaction into the activity.

03

Connect with the science

A short educational session connected the experience with tuberculosis prevention knowledge.

My contribution

I designed the interactive health-education event, bringing the story, game and educational session into one coherent experience with the team.

WHAT I TOOK FROM IT

A familiar activity format can make a technical topic approachable. The event documents an approach to science communication; it was not an evaluation of clinical or educational effectiveness.
