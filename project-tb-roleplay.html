A group photograph documents the activity; the poster and event text describe its design. The story and its missing medicine are fictional.

How could health education invite people to participate?

The activity used a fictional hospital mystery as an introduction to a health topic for participants without specialist training. The accompanying science session was intended to introduce tuberculosis awareness.

The format described in the event materials

01

Follow the story

The supplied booklet and event text describe a hospital mystery about a missing medicine.

02

Discuss the clues

The plan also lists a medical-themed social deduction game. This was an activity format, not clinical diagnostic training.

03

Introduce the health topic

A short science session was included in the event plan to introduce tuberculosis awareness and prevention.

My contribution

I helped plan the interactive health-education activity with the team, connecting the story, game and science session.

WHAT I TOOK FROM IT

The activity explored an interactive format for health communication. The supplied records do not establish improved medical knowledge, behaviour or health outcomes.
