The poster and outline document the theme and design. They are not treated as proof that every proposed segment took place or that all versions addressed the same audience.

How can personal experience become a thoughtful public conversation?

The poster invites discussion of discouraging messages in family life. A supporting draft outline proposes a reading-and-discussion format.

The format proposed in the facilitation outline

01

Introduce the theme

The draft proposes a brief introduction to discouraging language in family upbringing.

02

Read an excerpt together

Shared reading is proposed as a starting point for conversation.

03

Invite open discussion

The outline describes equal participation rather than an expert-led lecture. It is presented as a proposed format.

My contribution

I helped plan the discussion format with the team. The draft outline names me as the facilitator.

WHAT I TOOK FROM IT

A student-led reading discussion can consider personal experience while keeping a clear boundary from diagnosis, counselling or psychological treatment.
