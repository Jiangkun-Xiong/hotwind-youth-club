How can personal experience become a thoughtful public conversation?

The original poster invited participants to discuss discouraging messages experienced while growing up. It brought a familiar personal topic into a shared space for reflection.

How the idea took shape

01

Start from experience

The invitation centred on experiences of family and education.

02

Open a conversation

An open-discussion format gave participants room to consider different perspectives.

03

Reflect on growth

The topic connected everyday language with questions about how young people grow.

My contribution

I helped develop Hotwind’s programme of public discussions with the team. The original poster and supporting talk outline document this initiative.

WHAT I TOOK FROM IT

Student-led discussion can offer space for expression and reflection. It should remain clear about the boundary between peer dialogue and professional psychological care.
