The photographs record selected gatherings across two summers; posters show the way those gatherings were presented. No wellbeing or long-term relationship outcome is measured here.

How can a community make space for informal connection?

Alongside reading and structured discussions, Hotwind also made room for informal gatherings. The supplied photographs show people sharing a table, preparing the space and spending a summer evening together.

What the photographs record

01

Prepare the setting

A photograph shows a welcome board being prepared for a gathering.

02

Gather around a table

Photographs show participants seated together in indoor and outdoor settings.

03

Keep a record

The selected posters and photographs provide a record of activities in 2024 and 2025.

My contribution

As founder and team lead, I helped sustain the community and its activities with the core team. These gatherings are shown as collective work.

WHAT I TOOK FROM IT

A community also grows through ordinary moments: preparing a space, sitting at the same table and having time for one another.
